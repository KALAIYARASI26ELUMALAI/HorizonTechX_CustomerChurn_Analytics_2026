🔍 Key Insights

  • Month-to-month customers showed the highest churn rate at 42.71%.
  • Customers with 0–12 months tenure had the highest churn rate at 47.44%.
  • Electronic Check customers had a churn rate of 45.29%.
  • Fiber Optic customers showed a churn rate of 41.89%.
  • Customers with 4 risk factors had a churn rate of 71.16%.

🛠️ Technologies Used

💡 Business Recommendations

  • Focus retention campaigns on month-to-month customers.
  • Improve onboarding during the first 12 months.
  • Investigate churn among Fiber Optic customers.
  • Prioritize customers with 3–4 risk factors.
  • Create personalized retention offers for high-risk customers.
